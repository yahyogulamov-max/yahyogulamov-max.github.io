Privacy Policy — NurQuran

Application ID: uz.bato.nurquran · Developer: BATO · Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what information the NurQuran Android app ("the app") collects, why, and who it is shared with.

Information the app processes

  • Location. With your permission, the app uses your device location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. Coordinates are sent to prayer-time services (api.aladhan.com, islomapi.uz) and to OpenStreetMap Nominatim to show your city name. You can deny the permission and choose a city manually.
  • Google account (optional). If you sign in with Google, we store your name, e-mail address and reading progress (bookmarks, last page, khatm plan, settings) in Google Firebase so your progress syncs between devices. The app works fully without signing in.
  • Notifications. To send prayer-time and announcement notifications, the app stores a Firebase Cloud Messaging token for your device, your app language and the time you were last active.
  • Content downloads. Quran pages, audio recitations and translations are downloaded from public services (for example mp3quran.net, everyayah.com, alquran.cloud, quran.com). These services receive your IP address as part of a normal web request.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), app interactions and diagnostic data to show and measure ads, to prevent fraud and, where permitted, to show personalised ads. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown and can change their choice at any time in Profile → Ad privacy.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the services named above (Google Firebase, Google AdMob and the content/prayer-time services) to make the app work, or when required by law.

Security and retention

All data is transferred over encrypted HTTPS connections. Account data is kept while your account is in use. Data stored only on your device (bookmarks, settings, downloaded files) is removed when you uninstall the app.

Your choices and data deletion

You can use the app without signing in, deny location access, turn notifications off in the app settings and change ad consent in the Profile screen. To delete your account data, contact us using the details below and we will remove it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
